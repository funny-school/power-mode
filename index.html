FAQ

Does Power Mode read or save what I type?

No. Letters are only drawn on your screen for the effect and then disappear. Nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere, and password fields are always ignored. The only things saved – on your own computer – are your settings and three numbers: best combo, best WPM and words typed today.

Where does it work?

In text boxes on almost any website: search bars, chats, comments, emails, notes and forms. Chrome doesn't allow extensions on chrome:// pages or the Chrome Web Store.

Why don't I hear any sound?

Sounds are off by default. Click the Power Mode icon in your toolbar and pick a keyboard sound.

Can I turn it off on one website?

Yes – open the popup on that website and switch off "On for this site".

Is it free?

Yes, completely free.
