Power Mode

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Short version: Power Mode does not collect, store, sell or share anything you type or any personal information. Everything stays on your device.

What the extension does

Power Mode shows visual effects (sparks, a combo counter, a gentle shake) and optional keyboard sounds while you type in text fields on web pages.

What we do not collect

  • We never save, log or send the text you type. For the "Letters" effect a character is drawn on your screen for about one second and then discarded.
  • Password fields are always ignored.
  • No browsing history, no website content, no personal information, no analytics, no tracking.
  • No account is needed.

What is stored on your device

  • Your settings (spark style, sound, volume, shake, combo counter on/off and the list of websites where you turned Power Mode off), saved with Chrome's built-in storage so they can follow you between your own Chrome browsers.
  • Three numbers: your best combo, your best typing speed (WPM) and the number of words typed today. These stay in your browser and are never uploaded.

Network requests

The popup's optional "Take a break" card downloads a public list of free browser games (titles, pictures and links) once a day so it can suggest one. No information about you is sent with this request. A game page only opens if you click "Play".

Permissions

  • Read and change data on websites – needed so the effects can appear in text fields on the sites where you type.
  • Storage – saves your settings and the three numbers above.
  • Scripting – starts the effects in tabs that are already open right after you install, so you don't have to reload them.
  • Active tab – lets the popup know which website you're on so you can switch Power Mode off for it.

Removing your data

Click "Reset to default" in the popup to restore the default settings. Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored, including your records.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
